# What Texas Hold'em is

Texas Hold'em is the most played poker game in the world. Two to nine players sit at a table (in Taful, nearly always six), each with their own [chips](term:chips). Each deal of the cards is called a **hand**, and in every hand everyone plays for the [pot](term:pot): the chips bet in the middle of the table.

## Two cards of yours, five on the board

Every player gets two face-down cards, their [hole cards](term:hole-cards). Only you see yours. Then five cards are turned face up in the middle of the table, a few at a time. They make up the [board](term:board) and count for everyone.

```cards
Your cards: Jc 8c
Board: Jd 5c 2s Kh 7d
```

## The best five-card hand

At the end, each player makes the best **five-card** hand out of the seven they can use: their two and the five on the board. You may use both of your cards, just one, or none.

In the example above, your best hand is a pair of jacks, J♣ J♦, with K♥ 8♣ 7♦. The 5♣ and the 2♠ are left out:

```cards
Your best hand: Jc Jd Kh 8c 7d | ~5c ~2s
```

## Two ways to win

1. **At the [showdown](term:showdown)**: if the last betting round ends with two or more players, the cards are shown and the best five-card hand takes the pot.
2. **Alone**: if everyone else gives up (they [fold](term:fold)), whoever is left takes the pot at once, without showing anything.

That is why poker is not only about holding the best cards. You can win by making the others give up, and you can lose little by giving up early.

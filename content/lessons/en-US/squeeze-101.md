# The squeeze

A [squeeze](term:squeeze) is a 3-bet after one player opened and one or more players [cold-called](term:cold-call).

## Why it works

The caller usually has a medium hand: strong hands would have 3-bet. The opener cannot call wide either, because the caller still acts behind. Both players are squeezed between you and each other, and the pot already holds two raises' worth of chips.

## Sizing

Each caller adds one more open size. In Taful's tree a 3-bet is 3× the open in position and 3.75× out of position, plus 1× per caller. Squeezing from the big blind against a 2.5 bb open and one call is about 12 bb.

Bigger is necessary: there is more dead money in the pot, and you will be out of position against two ranges.

## Ranges

- **Value:** the usual 3-bet value hands, a little tighter, because one of two players might have a strong hand.
- **Bluffs:** hands with blockers and some playability, like suited aces.
- **Calls:** fewer than against a single open. A call in a three-way pot out of position is rarely good.

## Facing a squeeze

As the original opener, fold most of the range and continue with a strong one. As the cold caller, your range is capped (no premium hands), so you will fold a lot. That is a reason not to cold-call too often in the first place.

Drill it in spot mode with "Squeeze".

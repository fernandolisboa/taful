# Big blinds and effective stacks

Taful measures everything in **big blinds** (bb). At a $0.50/$1 table a $100 stack is 100 bb; at $1/$2 a $200 stack is also 100 bb. Thinking in [big blinds](term:big-blind) lets you compare spots across stakes.

## The pot before anyone acts

The blinds start the pot: 0.5 bb from the SB and 1 bb from the BB, 1.5 bb in total. When the button opens to 2.5 bb and the big blind calls, the pot on the flop is 2.5 + 2.5 + 0.5 = 5.5 bb (the small blind's 0.5 bb stays in).

## Effective stack

You can never win more from a player than the smaller of your two stacks. That smaller stack is the [effective stack](term:effective-stack). If you have 150 bb and your opponent has 80 bb, the effective stack is 80 bb: the extra 70 bb you hold do not matter in this hand.

The first slice of Taful plays **100 bb** cash games, the most common format online. Stack depth changes strategy a lot: the deeper the stacks, the more room there is to bet on later streets.

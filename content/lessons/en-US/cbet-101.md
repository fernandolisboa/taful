# The continuation bet

A [continuation bet](term:c-bet) (c-bet) is a bet on the flop by the player who made the last raise before the flop.

## Why c-bet

The preflop raiser usually has the stronger range, so a bet makes many hands fold, builds the pot with strong hands, and charges draws.

## How often

It depends on the [texture](term:texture) and the [range advantage](term:range-advantage):

- **High, dry boards in position** (K-7-2 rainbow, BTN vs BB): c-bet very often, with a small size, around a third of the pot.
- **Low, connected boards** (7-6-5, 8-6-4): c-bet much less. The defender has more strong hands, and a check keeps your range strong.
- **Out of position,** c-bet less than in position: you will face more raises and you will not close the action on later streets.
- **In 3-bet pots** the preflop raiser has a stronger range and a lower SPR, so small c-bets on many boards work.

## Which hands bet

When you bet with a high frequency and small size, you bet almost all of your range: value, draws, and some air with backdoor draws. When you bet less often, you bet:

- strong hands that want value and protection,
- good draws (semi-bluffs),
- and check medium hands that can call a bet and reach showdown.

## A mistake to avoid

C-betting every flop at the same size. A good opponent raises small c-bets on boards where you are weak and folds where you are strong. Taful's postflop spots show you the reference frequencies on every texture: compare them with your instincts.

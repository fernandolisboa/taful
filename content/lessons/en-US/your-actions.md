# Your actions

On your turn, you choose an action. Which ones you get depends on one question: **has anyone put chips into this round that you have not matched yet?**

## If nobody has bet

- **[Check](term:check)**: you stay in the hand without putting in chips, and the turn passes to the next player.
- **[Bet](term:bet)**: you put chips in, and the others will have to match them to stay in.

## If there is a bet to match

- **[Fold](term:fold)**: you throw your cards away and leave the hand. The chips you already put in stay in the pot.
- **[Call](term:call)**: you put in chips to match the bet.
- **[Raise](term:raise)**: you match the bet and put in more. Now the others have to match yours.

Check only exists when there is nothing to match. Preflop, the big blind already counts as a bet: whoever speaks before it calls 1 bb, raises or folds. Only the big blind itself can check, and only if nobody raised.

## All-in

[All-in](term:all-in) means betting every chip in your [stack](term:stack). You can do it on any turn. After that you make no more decisions in the hand: the remaining cards come out, and you play for the pot as far as your chips cover.

## An example

A round on the flop between three players:

1. The first player checks.
2. The second bets 3 bb.
3. The third raises to 9 bb.
4. The first folds.
5. The second calls, matching the 9 bb.

The round ends: the two players left put in 9 bb each, and the next card comes out.

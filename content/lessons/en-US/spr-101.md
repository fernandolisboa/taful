# Stack-to-pot ratio

The [stack-to-pot ratio](term:spr) (SPR) compares the [effective stack](term:effective-stack) behind with the pot on the flop: SPR = stack / pot.

## Examples at 100 bb

- The button opens to 2.5 bb and the big blind calls: the pot is 5.5 bb and 97.5 bb are left, an SPR of {{ calc: spr(stack=97.5, pot=5.5) | ratio }}.
- A 3-bet pot to 11 bb, called: the pot is about 22.5 bb with 89 bb behind, an SPR of {{ calc: spr(stack=89, pot=22.5) | ratio }}.
- A 4-bet pot to 25 bb, called: the pot is about 50.5 bb with 75 bb behind, an SPR of {{ calc: spr(stack=75, pot=50.5) | ratio }}.

## What SPR tells you

- **Low SPR (under 3):** top pair is usually strong enough to get all the money in. There is little room to bluff.
- **Medium SPR (3 to 6):** two-pair and sets want to build the pot; one pair needs care.
- **High SPR (above 6):** one pair rarely wants to play for stacks. Position, draws and hand reading matter most.

Plan the hand on the flop: with a low SPR, decide early whether you are willing to go all-in. With a high SPR, think about how many streets of value your hand can take.

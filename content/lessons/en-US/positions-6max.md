# Positions at a 6-max table

A 6-max table has six seats. The dealer [button](term:button) moves one seat clockwise every hand, and every seat's name is relative to it:

- **SB** (small blind) and **BB** ([big blind](term:big-blind)) post forced bets before the cards are dealt.
- **UTG** (under the gun) acts first before the flop.
- **HJ** (hijack) and **CO** (cutoff) come next.
- **BTN** (the button) acts last on every street after the flop.

## Why position matters

Before the flop the order is UTG, HJ, CO, BTN, SB, BB. From the flop on, the SB acts first and the BTN last. Acting last is called being **in [position](term:position)**: you see what your opponents do before you decide, you can check behind to see a free card, and you control the size of the pot.

That information is worth a lot. Good players open more hands from the button than from UTG, because UTG still has five players left to act behind, and any of them may hold a strong hand.

## A rule of thumb

The later your seat, the wider you can open. UTG opens a tight range; the button opens almost half of its hands. The blinds have already put chips in, but they play the rest of the hand out of position, so they defend carefully.

# Multiway pots

A [multiway](term:multiway) pot has three or more players after the flop. Strategy changes a lot.

## Someone usually has something

On any flop, one player hits often. With two opponents, the chance that at least one has a pair or a draw is much higher than against one. Your [fold equity](term:fold-equity) drops: a bluff must get through two players.

## Bet less, bet stronger

- **[C-bet](term:c-bet) much less.** The small, frequent c-bet that works heads-up is a mistake multiway.
- **Bet for value with real hands:** top pair with a good kicker and better.
- **Semi-bluff only with strong draws:** nut flush draws, open-enders with overcards.
- **Bluff very rarely.**

## Hand values change

- Top pair weak kicker loses value: someone may have it with a better kicker, or two-pair.
- Nut draws gain value: when they hit, more players can pay.
- Dominated hands (KJo, QTo) lose the most.

## Position matters more

In the middle of two players you can get raised from behind after you call. Being last to act is worth even more.

## In Taful

The first slice trains heads-up postflop spots, where the library has solved trees; full hands with three or more players after the flop end there. These principles help you when you play elsewhere.

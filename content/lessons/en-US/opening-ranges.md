# Opening ranges by position

When everyone before you folds, you can be the first to raise: an [open raise](term:rfi) (also called RFI, raise first in). At 6-max 100 bb Taful's charts open to 2.5 bb, and 3 bb from the small blind.

## How wide to open

Taful's reference chart opens about this often:

- **UTG:** about 17% of hands
- **HJ:** about 22%
- **CO:** about 28%
- **BTN:** about 39%
- **SB:** about 44% (raise or fold, no limps)

The pattern is [position](term:position): every player left to act behind you can wake up with a strong hand, and the players in the blinds will play the rest of the hand out of position against the button.

## What goes in

- **Every seat:** most pairs, strong aces, suited broadways (KQs, QJs, …).
- **Adding later:** suited aces down to A2s, suited connectors (87s, 76s), offsuit broadways (KJo, QTo), then weaker suited kings and queens.
- **Last to be added:** weak offsuit hands like K8o or J9o, mostly from the button and small blind.

Suited hands are worth more than they look: flushes, backdoor draws and the ability to bluff with equity.

## Why not limp?

Limping (just calling the big blind) gives up the chance to win the blinds straight away and lets the big blind see a flop for free. A raise wins the pot often and takes the initiative.

## Learn the edges

Nobody plays a chart perfectly from memory. Learn the edges: the weakest hands you open from each seat. Use spot mode in the Table screen with "Raise first in" to drill them.

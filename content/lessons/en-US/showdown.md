# Showdown: who takes the pot

## When there is a showdown

If two or more players are still in after the last bet on the river, the cards are shown: that is the [showdown](term:showdown). There is also a showdown when someone goes [all-in](term:all-in) and gets called before the river: the remaining cards come out, and then the hands are shown.

If at any moment everyone but one folds, there is no showdown. Whoever is left takes the pot and does not have to show their cards.

## Everyone makes their best five

Each player picks the best five cards out of their two and the five on the [board](term:board), and the hands are compared by the ranking.

```cards
Board: Kh Th 7c 4h 2h
You: Ah 3c
Opponent: Kd Ks
```

You use only one of your cards, the A♥, with the four hearts on the board: a flush. Your opponent uses both of theirs, and with the board's K♥ makes three kings. A flush beats three of a kind, and the pot is yours.

```cards
Your best hand: Ah Kh Th 4h 2h
Theirs: Kd Ks Kh | Th 7c
```

## Ties and split pots

When two players' best five cards have the same ranks, the hand is a tie and the pot is shared in equal parts: a [split pot](term:split-pot). Sometimes the best hand is all on the board and works for everyone:

```cards
Board: Ah Kd Qs Jc Tc
You: 6d 5h
Opponent: 9c 2d
```

The board already makes the highest straight there is, A K Q J T. Your opponent's 9♣ makes a lower straight, K Q J T 9, so it does not help. Both of you use the board's five cards and split the pot.

## Who shows first

On the river, if someone bet and got called, whoever made the last bet or raise shows first. If everyone checked, the first player to speak in the round shows first. In Taful the cards show up by themselves; at a live table, a player who sees they lost may throw their cards away without showing.

# Expected value

[Expected value](term:ev) (EV) is what a decision wins or loses on average if you could repeat it many times. Every grade in Taful is an EV comparison: the best action has the highest EV, and your EV loss is how far below it your choice was.

## The formula

Add up every outcome times its chance: EV = Σ probability × result. For a call with no more betting to come:

EV(call) = [equity](term:equity) × (pot + bet) − (1 − equity) × bet

where the pot is what was there before the bet.

- Pot 10 bb, the opponent bets 5 bb, you have 40% equity: EV = {{ calc: call_ev(equity=0.4, pot=10, bet=5) | bb(2) }}. A clear call.
- Same pot and bet with 25% equity: EV = {{ calc: call_ev(equity=0.25, pot=10, bet=5) | bb(2) }}. Exactly break-even: the [pot odds](term:pot-odds) ask for {{ calc: pot_odds(pot=10, bet=5) | pct(1) }}.
- With 15% equity: EV = {{ calc: call_ev(equity=0.15, pot=10, bet=5) | bb(2) }}. A fold (EV 0) is better.

## Folding is worth zero

Chips already in the pot are no longer yours. A fold always has EV 0 from this point on, so a call is right exactly when its EV is above zero, no matter how much you put in earlier.

## Results are not EV

You can make a +EV call and lose the hand, or a −EV call and win it. Judge decisions by EV, not by what the next card did. That is why Taful grades the decision when you make it, before any card is dealt.
